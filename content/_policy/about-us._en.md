## Our mission

The public’s one account for government.

Login.gov provides a simple, secure and private way for the public to access government websites. With one account and password, users can securely sign in to participating government websites and securely verify their identity.

By providing a consistent sign-in experience across participating websites and applications, Login.gov reduces the need to manage multiple usernames and passwords and helps agencies deliver simpler, more fraud-resistant, connected digital services.

## Our vision

The public is able to simply, securely, and privately log in to participating government websites and applications.

## About Login.gov

### Making government services simpler and safer for over a decade

- **December 18, 2015** - Congress established the requirement for a shared, trusted sign-on platform for public-facing federal websites requiring authentication. ([Source](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2015-title6/html/USCODE-2015-title6-chap6.htm))
- **May 2016** - USDS and GSA’s 18F team began implementation work on Login.gov. ([Source](https://www.usds.gov/report-to-congress/2016/shared-services/))
- **April 2017** - Login.gov launched as a common identity platform to improve and secure access to government online. ([Source](https://www.usds.gov/report-to-congress/2017/fall/login-dot-gov/))
- **Fall 2018** - Launch of identity verification
- **August 2022** - Reached 1 Million proofed accounts
- **October 9, 2024** - GSA announced certification and general availability of Login.gov’s IAL2 identity verification offering. ([Source](https://www.gsa.gov/about-gsa/newsroom/news-releases/gsas-logingov-announces-certification-of-ial2-10092024))
- **August 2026** - OMB directed expanded adoption of Login.gov as the default sign-in option across the Federal government ([Source](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/M-26-18-Scaling-Use-of-Login.gov-to-Deliver-a-Universal-Sign-on-for-Public-Services.pdf))

Login.gov was launched in 2017 in response to the Federal Cybersecurity Requirements statutory mandate ([6 USC § 1523 — Federal cybersecurity requirements, part (b)(1)(D)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title6/html/USCODE-2024-title6-chap6-subchapII-sec1523.htm)) that instructed agencies to “implement a single sign-on trusted identity platform for individuals accessing each public website of the agency that requires user authentication as developed by the Administrator of General Services” and “implement identity management…including multi-factor authentication.”

Initially developed by the General Services Administration and the U.S. Digital Service, the Login.gov program is operated as a division within the GSA’s Technology Transformation Services. The Login.gov team includes engineers, designers, user experience experts, and product managers with experience in digital identity and fraud preventions in the government and in the private sector. They collaborate to improve the product through public-private partnerships with best-in-class technologies; fine-tuning the user flow and design; incorporating the latest cybersecurity standards; and working with federal, state, and local partners to expand adoption.

As Login.gov’s role expands, its purpose remains consistent: making it simpler for people to securely access government services.
